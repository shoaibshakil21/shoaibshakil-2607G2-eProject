Who we are

About Beauty Of Beaches

We celebrate the beauty, diversity and wonder of the world's coastlines.

Our story

Inspiring people to explore the coast

Beauty Of Beaches is an educational travel-information website created to introduce visitors to famous beaches around the world. Our goal is to make discovering coastal destinations simple, enjoyable and inspiring.

We organize destinations into North, South, East and West zones, making it easier to browse places, view photographs and learn about each destination.

Our mission

Make discovery easy

To share helpful, accessible information about remarkable beaches and encourage responsible appreciation of nature.

Our vision

Celebrate every coastline

To become a welcoming starting point for people curious about the world's coastal beauty and diversity.

Our values

Curiosity and care

We value clear information, visual storytelling, respect for local communities and care for the environment.
